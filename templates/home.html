How it works

One account can be a customer, a provider and a manager. Pick a role to see its steps.

  1. Find a place. Search by name or browse by type and city.
  2. Join a queue. Pick a provider, or press Join Fastest and we pick the shortest wait.
  3. Watch it move. Your position and estimate refresh on their own.
  4. Rate the service. Optional, takes one tap.
  1. Link to your company. Give your manager your invite code.
  2. Start your shift. Choose the service you're offering.
  3. Call next. End the service or skip someone who didn't show.
  4. End your shift. Anyone still waiting is served before the queue closes.
  1. Register your company. Add category, location and opening hours.
  2. List your services. Customers can filter by them.
  3. Add providers. Enter each provider's invite code.
  4. Follow the queues. See live queues and each provider's history.
